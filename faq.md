# Shipping
Standard delivery takes 4-6 business days. Express delivery takes 2 business days.
Free shipping on orders above Rs 999. We ship across India.

# Returns
Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
Sale items cannot be returned. Refunds are processed in 5-7 business days.

# Exchanges
Size exchanges are free within 7 days of delivery, subject to stock.

# Payments
We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and cash on delivery.
Cash on delivery is available for orders up to Rs 5000.

# Order tracking
A tracking link is sent by SMS and email once your order is dispatched.

# Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled before dispatch from the My Orders page.

# Contact
Support is available 10 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday, at support@example.com.
